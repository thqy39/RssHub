Run it on your own server

A personal reading space for people who want to manage their own setup and keep the experience lightweight.

Jasper's RSS stores subscriptions and cached articles in a data.json file on the server where it runs. The site operator controls the data and can export the subscription list as OPML.

The interface supports English and Chinese. Desktop offers an article list and reader pane; mobile keeps the focus on the reading list.

Frequently asked questions

Which feed formats are supported?

Standard RSS and Atom feed URLs are supported.

Where are subscriptions and reading status stored?

They are stored in the data.json file on the server running Jasper's RSS and are managed by the site operator.

Can I move subscriptions from another reader?

Yes. Import an OPML subscription list, or export your current subscriptions to OPML.
